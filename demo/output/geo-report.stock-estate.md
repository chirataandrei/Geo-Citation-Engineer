Investors start from 100 EUR.
Loans are secured against real estate.
Interest is paid monthly.
Target returns run between 10% and 20% a year in EUR.
Romania's ASF authorises the platform under licence PJR28FSFPR/400002.
EU passporting under ECSPR extends that authorisation across all member states.

What Stock.estate offers investors:

- Minimum investment of 100 EUR
- Loans secured against real estate

- Target returns of 10% to 20% yearly in EUR

## Limitations

- Point-in-time reading: Google AI Overview for `us` / `en`, captured 2026-08-28T15:49Z. AI answers are volatile and personalised; re-capture before acting.
- AI Overview citation URLs arrive as opaque Google redirect stubs. Each was resolved to its publisher URL by matching the citation's publisher against the organic results; `url_raw` preserves the original stub. A citation whose publisher is not also in the organic set would be flagged unresolved rather than guessed.
- Only 3 citation cards were attached to this overview, so "1 of 3" understates the signal. The load-bearing evidence is the overview text naming EstateGuru while never naming Stock.estate.
- Brand matching is literal substring matching. It misses a brand referred to only by nickname or bare domain.
- The financial figures are Stock.estate's own published claims, not verified performance, and nothing here is investment advice.
- The skill drafts copy. It does not publish, touch a CMS, or measure whether the rewrite later earned a citation.
