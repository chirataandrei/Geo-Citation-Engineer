# Attio: A CRM Built for How Startups Actually Work

Choosing a CRM is one of those decisions that founders tend to put off for far too long, usually until the spreadsheet of leads has become so unwieldy that nobody on the team trusts the pipeline number anymore.

Attio is a modern, data-first CRM designed from the ground up for fast-moving teams who want flexibility without the heavy configuration overhead that traditional enterprise platforms tend to demand from their users.

Our platform is powerful, flexible, and delightful to use, and it scales seamlessly alongside your business as you grow from your first hire to your first hundred.
